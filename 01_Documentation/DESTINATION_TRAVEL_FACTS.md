### First-pass observations (no external lookup yet)

- **Hamburg vs Cologne (425 km each):** Hamburg is much further from Calais than
  Cologne, so identical distances can't both be right. Check both.
- **Berlin airport text is stale:** Tegel and Schönefeld have closed; everything
  is now BER (Berlin-Brandenburg). This wording should be updated regardless of
  the distance check.
- **Several Major-Cities pages have no transfer time** (Cologne, Dresden,
  Düsseldorf, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Hannover) — inconsistent with the rest of the
  site, which all quote one.
- **Three Mosel pages have no Calais distance** (Bernkastel-Kues, Cochem, Daun).
- **Dresden → Hamburg airport** is an unusually long transfer; Dresden or Berlin
  may be the intended arrival airport.
- Minor typo: Regensburg reads "1 hours 30 mins".
